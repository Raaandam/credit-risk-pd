## 1. Purpose and scope

The model estimates the probability that a 36-month unsecured retail instalment loan
defaults (is charged off) over its life, using only information available at
application. PDs feed Expected Loss and a portfolio credit-VaR engine used for capital
and concentration analysis. Out of scope: LGD/EAD modelling beyond grade-level
averages, pricing, and collections strategies.

### 3.4 Portfolio risk

- **LGD:** empirical workout LGD = 1 - recoveries / principal outstanding at default,
  averaged by grade over development-window defaults.
- **EAD:** outstanding principal (`<<RUNTIME: ead_method>>`).
- **PD:** champion lifetime PD converted to a 1-year PD at constant hazard, used for EL,
  VaR and IRB comparison (horizon: <<RUNTIME: pd_horizon>>).
- **EL** = PD x LGD x EAD.
- **Credit VaR:** one-factor Gaussian copula with an additional sector (loan purpose)
  factor, <<RUNTIME: n_paths>> Monte Carlo paths, rho = <<RUNTIME: rho>>, sector loading = <<RUNTIME: sector_rho>>;
  default counts per (sector x PD band) cell drawn binomially to retain granularity risk.
- **Benchmark:** Vasicek ASRF closed form at the same rho, and Basel IRB other-retail
  capital formula.

## 4. Key assumptions

1. Default behaviour of 2013-2015 vintages is representative of 2016 originations.
2. Lifetime PD converts to a 1-year PD under a constant hazard rate.
3. LGD is constant within grade and independent of the systematic factor (no downturn LGD).
4. Asset correlation is homogeneous (<<RUNTIME: rho>>) plus a uniform sector loading; correlations
   are expert-set, not estimated.
5. EAD equals outstanding principal; there are no undrawn commitments.

## 5. Limitations

1. **Censoring:** excluding unresolved loans over-represents early outcomes in the 2016
   vintage; OOT default rates may be biased relative to the full lifetime.
2. **Reject inference:** the model is built on accepted loans only, so performance on
   the through-the-door population is unknown.
3. **Recovery lag:** recent charge-offs may have incomplete recoveries, overstating LGD (conservative).
4. **Protected attributes** are not available; fairness checks rely on proxies.
5. **Correlation parameters** are assumptions; VaR is sensitive to them (see stress tests).
6. **Back-test independence:** binomial/Jeffreys tests ignore default correlation and are
   therefore strict in stressed years.
